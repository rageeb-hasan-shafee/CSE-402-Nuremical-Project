Slide 1 • Mathematical Physics & Problem Formulation

Gravitational N-Body Problem & Hamiltonian Invariants

Base Reference: Tailin Zhu (2020), N-body Simulations of the Solar System with CPU-based Parallel Methods, University of Bristol.

Physical Conservation Laws

An isolated gravitational system is governed by a Hamiltonian $H = T(\mathbf{p}) + V(\mathbf{q})$. It possesses 10 classical integrals of motion: Total Energy ($H$), Total Linear Momentum ($\mathbf{P}$), Center of Mass vector, and Total Angular Momentum ($\mathbf{L}$).

Gaussian Astronomical System

Units chosen to eliminate round-off catastrophic cancellation: Length in $\text{AU}$, time in days, mass in Solar Masses ($M_\odot$). Gravitational constant $G \approx 2.9591220828559 \times 10^{-4}\text{ AU}^3 M_\odot^{-1}\text{ day}^{-2}$. Point masses use exact Plummer softening $\epsilon = 0.0$.

Slide 2 • Numerical Analysis & Symplectic Geometry

Symplectic Invariance vs. Energy Dissipation

Velocity Verlet (Symplectic Map)

Preserves the Poincaré 2-form $d\mathbf{p} \wedge d\mathbf{q}$ and phase-space volume (Liouville's theorem). While instantaneous energy oscillates slightly, it stays locked in a bounded band ($|\Delta E / E_0| < 10^{-7}$) over millions of steps without secular drift.

Forward Euler & RK4 Failure Mode

Non-symplectic integrators introduce artificial numerical dissipation or energy pumping ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{f} \neq 0$). Forward Euler expands phase space, continuously injecting energy and causing planets to spiral outward to infinity. Standard RK4 bleeds energy, causing orbits to decay.

General Relativity 1PN Precession

Incorporates Einstein's First Post-Newtonian correction. Our simulation measures Mercury's perihelion advance at 42.983''/century (0.007% error vs analytic 42.980''/century).

Slide 3 • Hardware Sympathy & Memory Architecture

Vectorization, Zero Allocations & Microarchitecture

Structure-of-Arrays (SoA)

Coordinates separated into contiguous `x`, `y`, `z`, `m` arrays. Allows CPU/GPU memory controllers to stream contiguous 64-byte cachelines directly into AVX2 / SIMD registers with 100% bus utilization.

Zero Heap Allocations

All buffers allocated once at startup. Time-stepping loop operates strictly in-place or via $O(1)$ pointer swaps. Completely eliminates OS memory manager locks and TLB thrashing during execution.

Heterogeneous Hybrid P/E Cores

Intel i5-1340P has 4 fast P-cores and 8 slower E-cores. `static` schedule stalls at barriers waiting for E-cores; `dynamic` work-stealing delivers a 1.45× speedup by balancing chunk dispatch.

Newton's 3rd Law Pairwise Halving

Using $\mathbf{F}_{ji} = -\mathbf{F}_{ij}$ slashes pairwise calculations from $N(N-1)$ to $\frac{N(N-1)}{2}$, halving square root operations. Thread-private force accumulation buffers eliminate race conditions without atomics.

Supervisor Oral Defense Quick-Reference

Q: Why does OpenMP run SLOWER than serial execution at N = 100? ZHU (2020) FIG. 4
At $N=100$, force computation takes only $\approx 0.035\text{ ms}$. Thread spawning, `#pragma omp parallel` barrier synchronization, and cache-coherency ping-pong exceed the arithmetic payload. Parallelism only amortizes above $N \ge 500$.
Q: Why is FP32 28× faster than FP64 on the NVIDIA RTX 5090? CUDA HARDWARE
Consumer GeForce GPUs are engineered for graphics and AI, with a hardware FP64:FP32 ALU ratio of 1:64. Only datacenter GPUs (A100/H100) feature 1:2 ratios. Hence, single-precision achieves $28\times$ higher throughput ($0.556\text{ ms}$ at $N=10,000$).
Q: In MPI, why does `allgather` scale better than `master-worker`? DISTRIBUTED MPI
In `master-worker`, rank 0 gathers all coordinates via `MPI_Gatherv` and broadcasts via `MPI_Bcast`, creating a serialized bandwidth bottleneck at rank 0. `allgather` uses decentralized ring topology where all ranks communicate in parallel.
